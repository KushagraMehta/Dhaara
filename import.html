Playback and Links

Dhaara pauses YouTube before it starts Spotify, and pauses Spotify when a video starts. The first time a song opens in the Spotify app, your browser asks to open Spotify. Tick Always Allow.

Keyboard Shortcuts

To change them, paste chrome://extensions/shortcuts into the address bar.
The browser does not let an extension open that page. There you can also make a shortcut global, so it works while another app is in front.
